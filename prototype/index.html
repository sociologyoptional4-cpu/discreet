Two fictional adult personas let you play both sides of a request. Network timing is simulated so you can see pending, sent and failed states.

What makes it different

Swipe apps reward volume. Nearby Chat rewards intent: every request carries a reason, every chat opens only after a yes, and getting from a chat to an actual meet-up is built in and safe.

Tonight

  • Say you’re free now: “Jazz night, anyone?”
  • Disappears in 24 hours
  • “Free tonight” filter and ring on avatars

Requests with a reason

  • Reply to a profile prompt
  • The recipient sees the quote and your note
  • The note opens the chat when accepted

The Drop

  • Up to 6 people every evening at 7 pm
  • Ranked by shared interests
  • Limited on purpose, so each one gets real attention

Icebreakers

  • This or that, Would you rather, Rapid fire
  • Answered with one tap
  • Ends the “hey” problem

Plan cards

  • Suggest a public place and time
  • Both confirm
  • Share with a trusted friend; check-in reminder

Spark

  • Five bars that grow as you both reply
  • No streak punishment, no guilt
  • Lives in the chat header, not the inbox

Colour language

Each colour means one thing, so vibes read at a glance. Colour never carries meaning alone: every state also has a label, icon or count. Text pairs on these colours measure at least 4.5:1 (lowest 4.55:1, computed).
